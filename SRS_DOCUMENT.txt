1. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

1.1 PURPOSE
-----------
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document details the complete
functional and non-functional specifications for the Automotive Service Management
System. The system is designed to digitize, streamline, and govern all dealership
aftersales operations, electric vehicle (EV) maintenance workflows, quotations,
billing receipts, inspection sign-offs, customer reminders, and multi-branch
business analytics for authorized automotive distributors (specifically configured
for BYD Cambodia and DENZA).

This document serves as the foundational technical agreement between business
stakeholders, service advisors, workshop engineers, quality assurance personnel,
software architects, and maintenance developers.


1.2 SCOPE OF THE SYSTEM
-----------------------
The Automotive Service Management System is a centralized, web-based enterprise
application that replaces manual paper receipts, disconnected spreadsheets, and
fragmented messaging.

Core Capabilities & Operational Scope:
- Role-Based Authentication & Operational Boundaries: Access control strictly
  distinguishing System Administrators from Service Advisors (SAs). SAs are
  physically constrained to exactly 1 workshop branch, while logically permitted
  to service up to 2 automotive brands (BYD and DENZA) supported at that branch.
- Multi-Brand & Multi-Branch Workshop Management: Hierarchical structuring of
  automotive brands (BYD, DENZA) and physical service workshops across
  geographical locations (Chroy Changva 6A, Phnom Penh Flagship, Siem Reap Center,
  City Mall Hub), including dual-brand certified facilities.
- Prefix & Numbering Isolation Engine: Deterministic, non-guessing sequence
  generation isolating document counters by active brand prefix ({PREFIX}-QT...
  for quotations and {PREFIX}60M-... for receipts).
- Dynamic Intake UX & Document Letterhead Switching: Prominent top brand context
  toggles for dual-brand SAs with instant sequence preview recalculation and
  letterhead swapping; single-brand SAs operate under fixed brand locks.
- Quotation Lifecycle Engine: Drafting, pricing, tax computation, versioning,
  status tracking, and 1-click conversion into active Service Receipts.
- Service Receipt & Work Order Processing: Vehicle intake parameters, battery
  State-of-Charge (SoC) tracking, spare parts/labor fee schedules, old parts
  disposition, and multi-step workshop sign-offs (Technician, QA Inspector,
  Assistant Manager, Accounting, Delivery Person, Customer).
- Scoped Operational Table Views: Automatic scoping of quotation and receipt
  lists to the SA's assigned workshop facility, equipped with top-level brand
  filtering tabs (All Brands | BYD | DENZA).
- Automated Telegram Bot Messaging: Instant dispatch of formatted repair reminders
  and customer notices to designated Telegram service channels.
- Customer & Vehicle Directory: Automatic aggregation and tracking of customer
  profiles, vehicle models, plate numbers, VINs, service histories, and cumulative
  spending.
- Executive Analytics & Business Intelligence: Interactive dashboards displaying
  revenue trends, monthly/weekly performance, workshop output comparisons, and
  advisor rankings using responsive charts.
- Financial Reporting & Export Engine: Granular transaction reporting with multiple
  date ranges, brand/branch filtering, CSV data export, and print summaries.
- High-Fidelity Document Printing & PDF Generation: Factory-grade A4 portrait
  document generation matching factory layout standards, with bilingual headings
  (Khmer & English), dual-logo support, and isolated iframe print handling.
- Bilingual Interface & Keyboard Navigation: Real-time English/Khmer localization
  and a universal command palette (Ctrl+K / Cmd+K).

   - Top-level brand filtering tabs ([ All Brands ] | [ BYD ] | [ DENZA ]).

   - Direct Telegram Bot notification dispatch for scheduled service reminders.

7. Intelligence & Financial Reporting:

2.3 USER CLASSES, OPERATIONAL ROLE CONSTRAINTS & CHARACTERISTICS
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. System Administrator:
   - Target Users: Workshop General Managers, Operations Directors, IT Admins.
   - Scope: Global system access across all physical branches and automotive brands.
   - Capabilities: Creation/editing of brands, branches, staff profiles, system settings,
     activity logs, financial reports, quotations, receipts, and global dashboards.

2. Service Advisor (SA):
   - Target Users: Front-desk service advisors, workshop receptionists, service writers.
   - Operational Role Constraints:
     * Exactly 1 Physical Branch: An SA is physically employed on a specific workshop floor
       (e.g., Chroy Changva 6A). The SA can NEVER be assigned to multiple branches.
     * Up to 2 Brands: An SA can service 1 or up to 2 vehicle lines (BYD and/or DENZA)
       operating out of their assigned workshop facility.
     * Branch-Brand Affinity: The brands assigned to an SA MUST be a subset of the brands
       supported by the SA's branch. If Branch X only supports BYD, the SA cannot service DENZA.
   - Operational Permissions:
     * Quotation & Receipt authoring, editing, status tracking, and printing.
     * Automatic scoping of list views to their assigned branch.
     * Brand context selection via header toggle for authorized dual-brand operations.
     * Restricted from system settings, staff management, and branch/brand administration.

2.5 DESIGN & IMPLEMENTATION CONSTRAINTS
---------------------------------------
1. Physical vs. Logical Boundaries:
   A physical branch (e.g. Chroy Changva 6A) represents a singular physical building,
   workshop floor, and tool bay. However, logically it can house dual-brand operations
   servicing both BYD and DENZA vehicles. The system MUST maintain this distinction:
   physical assets (branch location, contact info, staff presence) are singular, while
   logical assets (brand logos, prefixes, legal entity names, VAT certificates) are partitioned.

2. Branch-Brand Affinity Constraint:
   The system MUST strictly validate that an SA cannot be assigned to an automotive brand
   that their assigned branch does not support. Validation must execute both on frontend
   staff management forms and backend database update endpoints.

3. Prefix & Numbering Isolation:
   Document numbering must never guess or share sequence sequences across brands.
   Quotation numbers ({PREFIX}-QT{YYMM}-{SEQ}) and Receipt numbers ({PREFIX}{YYMM}-{SEQ})
   must maintain independent, monotonic auto-incrementing counters isolated strictly
   by the brand prefix.

4. Document Print Fidelity:
   Printed receipts and quotations must strictly fit an A4 portrait canvas (210mm x 297mm)
   with 190mm content width and zero vertical clipping across all pages.

5. Unicode Khmer Typography:
   All official printed headers and UI elements must render Unicode Khmer fonts
   (e.g., Kantumruy Pro / Battambang) without character overlap, clipping, or font fallback failures.

- Automotive brands BYD and DENZA operate with distinct legal entity titles and prefixes.

3.1 USER INTERFACES
-------------------
1. Intake Form Dynamic Scope (CreateQuotation.tsx & CreateReceipt.tsx):
   - Single-Brand SA Mode: When an SA is assigned exactly 1 brand, the brand dropdown
     is locked/read-only with an explicit indicator badge. The branch is locked to the SA's branch.
   - Dual-Brand SA Mode: When an SA is assigned 2 brands (BYD and DENZA), the form renders
     a prominent, high-contrast segmented toggle banner at the very top:
     +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
     |  ACTIVE BRAND CONTEXT:   [ (o) BYD ]   |   [ ( ) DENZA ] |
     +--------------------------------------------------------------------+
   - Real-Time Reactive Re-scoping:
     * Clicking the alternate brand toggle immediately recalculates the sequence preview
       (e.g. dynamically switching from 'BYD-QT2609-001' to 'DENZA-QT2609-001').
     * The document letterhead, company legal name, VAT registration, address, and
       logo immediately swap to the selected brand context.
     * All previously entered customer details, vehicle parameters, and fee items are
       strictly preserved without data reset.

2. Scoped Table Views (QuotationList.tsx & ReceiptList.tsx):
   - Default Branch Scoping: Service Advisors opening Quotation or Receipt lists are
     automatically scoped to their assigned branch floor (e.g. 'BYD Chroy Changva 6A').
   - Top Brand Filter Tabs: Positioned at the top of the table header:
     [ All Brands (N) ]  |  [ BYD (N) ]  |  [ DENZA (N) ]
     The tabs restrict filtering to the brands assigned to the active SA.
   - Administrator Override: Administrators retain full unconstrained dropdown controls
     to inspect all branches and all brands nationwide.

3. Design Language & Visual Tokens:
   - Primary BYD Red: #E31B23
   - Primary DENZA Blue: #1E40AF / #3B82F6
   - Slate Neutral Palette: #0F172A (Text), #F8FAFC (App Background), #FFFFFF (Surfaces)
   - Border Radii: rounded-xl (12px) and rounded-2xl (16px)

4. SYSTEM FEATURES & DETAILED FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

4.1 AUTHENTICATION & ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC)
-----------------------------------------------------
- REQ-AUTH-001 (User Login):
  Users authenticate using email and password credentials.
- REQ-AUTH-002 (Role Differentiation):
  Two distinct roles: 'Admin' (full enterprise authority) and 'Service Advisor' (branch operations).
- REQ-AUTH-003 (Role & User Switching):
  Fast user switching in development/admin mode to audit varying staff permission profiles.
- REQ-AUTH-004 (SA 1-Branch Assignment):
  A Service Advisor user profile MUST be linked to exactly one physical branch ID (branch_id).
- REQ-AUTH-005 (SA Up-to-2 Brands Assignment):
  A Service Advisor profile contains assigned_brand_ids: string[], permitting 1 or up to 2
  brand assignments, constrained to the brands supported by the assigned branch.
- REQ-AUTH-006 (Branch-Brand Affinity Enforcement):
  The system MUST reject any user creation or modification that assigns a brand to an SA
  which is not contained in the selected branch's supported_brand_ids.
- REQ-AUTH-007 (Session Persistence):
  Active sessions and selected active brand context persist across browser refreshes.


4.2 MULTI-BRAND & MULTI-BRANCH WORKSHOP MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------
- REQ-ORG-001 (Brand Management):
  Admin interface to create and update brand profiles: id, brand_code, brand_name,
  logo_type, logo_url, service_center_name, local_company_name, address, telephone,
  email, document_prefix, receipt_prefix, status.
- REQ-ORG-002 (Branch Physical & Logical Modeling):
  Branches represent physical facilities with supported_brand_ids: string[] and is_dual_brand: boolean.
- REQ-ORG-003 (Dual-Brand Branch Capability):
  A branch may be configured as a dual-brand facility (e.g. Chroy Changva 6A supporting
  both 'brand-byd' and 'brand-denza'), allowing SAs bound to that branch to service both makes.
- REQ-ORG-004 (Cascading Branch Selection):
  In administrative views, selecting a brand filters available branches to those supporting that brand.


4.3 QUOTATION MANAGEMENT LIFECYCLE
----------------------------------
- REQ-QTN-001 (Quotation Number Generation):
  Generates sequential, branded quotation numbers: {PREFIX}-QT{YYMM}-{SEQ} (e.g. BYD-QT2609-001).
- REQ-QTN-002 (Prefix & Numbering Isolation):
  Quotation sequence counters MUST be calculated independently per brand prefix.
  A quotation for BYD must never advance or increment the sequence counter for DENZA.
- REQ-QTN-003 (Dynamic Intake Scope):
  When creating a quotation, if the SA has 2 assigned brands, display a prominent top toggle
  [ BYD ] | [ DENZA ]. Switching the toggle immediately recalculates the quotation sequence preview.
- REQ-QTN-004 (Letterhead Swapping):
  Toggling brand context swaps company name, VAT number, address, and logo on the quotation canvas.
- REQ-QTN-005 (Fee Items Matrix):
  Dynamic line items for spare parts and labor with real-time Line Amount, Subtotal, VAT, and Total.
- REQ-QTN-006 (Quotation Statuses):
  Supported statuses: Draft, Quotation, Pending, Accepted, Converted, Cancelled, Expired.


4.4 QUOTATION-TO-RECEIPT CONVERSION ENGINE
------------------------------------------
- REQ-CNV-001 (One-Click Conversion):
  Single-click action to convert an Accepted quotation into an official Service Receipt.
- REQ-CNV-002 (Data & Brand Preservation):
  Transfers all customer data, vehicle parameters, fee items, and selected brand/branch
  identities without re-entry or manual selection.
- REQ-CNV-003 (Traceability Linkage):
  The created receipt retains quotation_id, and source quotation status updates to 'Converted'.


4.5 SERVICE RECEIPT & EV REPAIR ORDER PROCESSING
------------------------------------------------
- REQ-RCP-001 (Receipt Number Generation):
  Generates branded receipt numbers adhering to isolated brand rules (e.g. BYD60M-2609-001
  vs DENZA60M-2609-001).
- REQ-RCP-002 (Intake Form Dynamic Scope):
  If the SA has 2 assigned brands, render top toggle [ BYD ] | [ DENZA ]. Switching recalculates
  receipt number preview and updates letterhead branding. If 1 brand, lock the brand context.
- REQ-RCP-003 (EV Intake Parameters):
  Records battery State of Charge (SoC %), in_time, out_time, buy_time, and estimated completion date.
- REQ-RCP-004 (Custody & Parts Disposition):
  Tracks is_owner, is_sender, old_parts_action ('Take away' | 'Give up'), and warehouse keeper.
- REQ-RCP-005 (Multi-Stage Handover Sign-offs):
  8-stage sign-off chain: Technician, QA Inspector, Assistant Manager, Accounting, Delivery Person, Customer.


4.6 SCOPED TABLE VIEWS & OPERATIONAL FILTERING
----------------------------------------------
- REQ-FLT-001 (Default SA Branch Scoping):
  When a Service Advisor opens QuotationList.tsx or ReceiptList.tsx, the list automatically
  defaults to records belonging to the SA's assigned branch_id.
- REQ-FLT-002 (Top Brand Segmented Filter):
  Quotation and Receipt lists provide top-level segmented filter tabs:
  [ All Brands ] | [ BYD ] | [ DENZA ] (restricted to the SA's assigned brands).
- REQ-FLT-003 (Admin Nationwide View):
  Administrators have unconstrained access to filter by any branch or brand across the country.


4.7 TELEGRAM NOTIFICATIONS & REMINDERS
--------------------------------------
- REQ-TEL-001 (Automated Dispatch):
  Instant dispatch of maintenance reminders and customer repair notices to Telegram channels.


4.8 CUSTOMER & VEHICLE INTELLIGENCE DIRECTORY
---------------------------------------------
- REQ-DIR-001 (Dynamic Synthesis):
  Aggregates unique customer and vehicle profiles across all quotations and receipts with
  cumulative service history, visit frequency, and lifetime spend.


4.9 EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD & REAL-TIME ANALYTICS
---------------------------------------------
- REQ-DSH-001 (KPIs & Revenue Trends):
  Real-time metrics: Total Revenue, Active Quotations, Completed Receipts, and Branch Breakdown.


4.10 A4 PRINTING & HIGH-FIDELITY PDF GENERATION

  Generates factory-compliant A4 portrait documents (190mm content width) with bilingual

5.2 DATA DICTIONARY
-------------------
1. BRAND ENTITY (Brand):
- id (String, PK, Unique): Brand unique identifier (e.g. 'brand-byd', 'brand-denza')
- brand_code (String, Required): e.g. 'BYD', 'DENZA'
- brand_name (String, Required): e.g. 'BYD', 'DENZA'
- logo_type (String): 'byd' | 'denza' | 'custom'
- logo_url (String): Custom logo image URL
- service_center_name (String): Branded workshop header
- local_company_name (String): Official Khmer legal entity title
- address (String): Legal headquarters address
- telephone, email (String): Contact credentials
- document_prefix (String): e.g. 'BYD', 'DENZA'
- receipt_prefix (String): e.g. 'BYD60M', 'DENZA60M'
- status (String): 'Active' | 'Inactive'

2. BRANCH ENTITY (Branch):
- id (String, PK, Unique): Branch unique identifier (e.g. 'b-byd-6a')
- branch_code (String, Required): Short code ('6A', 'SR', 'CM', 'PP')
- branch_name (String, Required): Physical facility name (e.g. 'BYD Chroy Changva 6A')
- service_center_name (String): Specific center display name
- supported_brand_ids (Array<String>, Required): Brand IDs serviced at this facility
  (e.g. ['brand-byd'] for single brand, or ['brand-byd', 'brand-denza'] for dual-brand)
- is_dual_brand (Boolean): True if branch services multiple brands
- address (String): Physical workshop address
- telephone, email (String): Workshop front-desk contact
- status (String): 'Active' | 'Inactive'

3. USER ENTITY (User):
- id (String, PK, Unique): Staff ID (e.g. 'u-1')
- name (String, Required): Full staff name
- email (String, Required): Login email address
- password (String): Authentication credential
- role (String, Required): 'Admin' | 'Service Advisor'
- branch_id (String, FK, Required for SA): Exactly 1 bound physical workshop branch
- branch (String): Display name of assigned branch
- assigned_brand_ids (Array<String>, Required for SA): 1 to 2 brand IDs serviced by SA.
  Validation: Must be a non-empty subset of Branch.supported_brand_ids.
- active_brand_id (String, FK): Preselected/active brand context for document authoring
- default_sa (String): Default advisor signature string
- status (String): 'Active' | 'Inactive'

4. QUOTATION ENTITY (Quotation):
- id (String, PK, Unique): Quotation unique identifier
- quotation_no (String, Required): Branded sequence (e.g. BYD-QT2609-001)
- brand_id (String, FK): Active brand context at creation
- branch_id (String, FK): Physical workshop where quotation was issued
- customer_name, phone, plate_no, vehicle_model, color, vin, battery (String)
- mileage (Number): Vehicle odometer in km
- fee_items (Array<FeeItem>): Item descriptions, SAP numbers, quantities, prices, line totals
- subtotal, vat, total_amount (Number): Real-time financial calculations
- status (String): 'Draft' | 'Quotation' | 'Pending' | 'Accepted' | 'Converted' | 'Cancelled' | 'Expired'
- created_by, created_by_name (String): Author SA information
- created_date, remind_date (String): ISO date strings

5. SERVICE RECEIPT ENTITY (Receipt):
- Inherits all vehicle, customer, fee item, and financial fields from Quotation
- receipt_no (String, Required): Branded sequence (e.g. BYD60M-2609-001)
- quotation_id (String, Optional): Source quotation linkage
- in_time, out_time, buy_time, estimated_repaired_date (String)
- is_owner, is_sender (Boolean), old_parts_action ('Take away' | 'Give up')
- completion (Object): 8-stage sign-offs (technician, qa, assistant manager, accounting, delivery, customer)
- status (String): 'Pending' | 'Completed' | 'Delivered' | 'Cancelled'


5.3 PREFIX & NUMBERING ISOLATION ALGORITHM
------------------------------------------
To ensure zero sequence collisions between brands:
1. For Quotations:
   - Identify active brand prefix: Prefix = `${brandCode}-QT${yy}${mm}-`
   - Filter existing quotations matching Prefix strictly:
     matches = quotations.filter(q => q.quotation_no.startsWith(Prefix))
   - Next Sequence = Max(matches.sequence) + 1
   - Return `${Prefix}${String(NextSequence).padStart(3, '0')}`
2. For Service Receipts:
   - Identify active receipt prefix: Prefix = `${receiptPrefix}${yy}${mm}-`
   - Filter existing receipts matching Prefix strictly:
     matches = receipts.filter(r => r.receipt_no.startsWith(Prefix))
   - Next Sequence = Max(matches.sequence) + 1
   - Return `${Prefix}${String(NextSequence).padStart(3, '0')}`
